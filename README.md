## Overview

Lockr introduces a different way to launch tokens.

Instead of creator rewards being extracted and sold, they are redirected into reward pools that benefit holders willing to commit for the long term.

Every token launched through Lockr automatically receives its own reward pool.

Holders simply lock their tokens for a chosen duration and instantly receive SOL based on:

- Amount locked
- Lock duration
- Available rewards in the pool

When the lock expires, users receive their original tokens back.

No selling.
No staking rewards over time.
Just instant liquidity.

---

## How it works

### 1. Launch a token

Projects launch through Lockr.

During launch, creator rewards are automatically allocated to the token's reward pool.

---

### 2. Reward pool grows

Instead of rewards being withdrawn by creators, they accumulate inside the pool.

The pool becomes available to long-term holders.

---

### 3. Lock tokens

Holders choose:

- Token amount
- Lock duration

The protocol calculates the upfront SOL reward.

---

### 4. Receive SOL instantly

Users receive their SOL immediately.

Their tokens remain locked until the chosen unlock date.

---

### 5. Unlock

After the lock period ends, users reclaim their original tokens.
